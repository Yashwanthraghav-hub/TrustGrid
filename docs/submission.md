## Submission description

TrustGrid helps a community turn noisy multilingual crisis reports into human-reviewed needs, compare transparent allocation choices against confirmed supply, and track assistance through source, volunteer, and recipient handoffs. AI organizes report text but never verifies it or controls stock. PostgreSQL row locks, RLS, organization-scoped references, an append-only ledger, and replay-resistant confirmations make the demo's accounting auditable.
